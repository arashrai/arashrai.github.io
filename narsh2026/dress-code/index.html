Dress Code

Mehndi & Jago

Thursday, September 24 · Lakeside Home, Peachland

We'll be celebrating at a lakeside house in Peachland. Natalie, Arash, and family will be wearing traditional Indian outfits. Everyone is welcome to wear traditional clothes or whatever feels slightly dressed up and comfortable for an outdoor lakeside party (colorful semi-formal / dressy casual).

Good to know: We will be outdoors on the lawn by the water, so walkable shoes (flats, wedges, or block heels) are recommended for women.

The Anand Karaj

The wedding ceremony · Okanagan Sikh Gurdwara

We warmly recommend wearing traditional Punjabi clothing: a kurta pajama for men and a salwar kameez for women.

If you'd prefer to wear Western attire, no worries! Formal or dressy clothing is great. Just make sure it is respectful for inside the Gurdwara:

  • Shoulders and legs must be covered
  • Wear a head covering that covers most of your hair

If you don't have your own head covering, don't worry! Head coverings will be provided for everyone at the Gurdwara.

Good to know: Shoes will come off inside the temple, and during the ceremony you'll be seated on carpeted ground, so choose something you'll be comfortable sitting in.

The Reception

Saturday evening · Hillcrest Farm Market

Formal or Cocktail Attire. Suits, evening dresses, and formal Indian wear in any color.

Good to know: Grass-friendly footwear is recommended for the lawn venue.
