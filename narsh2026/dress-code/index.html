Dress Code

The Anand Karaj

The wedding ceremony · Okanagan Sikh Gurdwara

We warmly recommend wearing Punjabi-style clothing. For women, a shalwar kameez-style outfit is perfect.

If you'd prefer not to wear Punjabi-style clothing, please just make sure to:

  • Cover your shoulders and legs
  • Wear a head covering that covers most of your hair

If you don't have your own head covering, don't worry — some will be provided at the Gurdwara. Note that we'll be taking shoes off.

Good to know: During the ceremony you'll be seated on carpeted ground, so choose something you'll be comfortable sitting in.

Everything Else

Feel free to wear whatever suits you best! We'd love to see lots of colour, so please steer away from black and grey.

Otherwise, every colour is welcome — the brighter and more joyful, the better.
